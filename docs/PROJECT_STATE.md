## ACCEPTED LIMITATIONS (documented, deliberately not changed)
- `is_gift` and `shield_member` are not restricted to Y/N by the API; unseen values are accepted by the model.
- The feature exclusion guard is redundant with the fixed 9-column selection; the real controls are the explicit `usecols` list and that selection.
- `score.py` asserts exactly 2,096 rows because it produces the assessment's required `predictions.csv`; it is not a general batch scorer.
- A corrupt or incompatible artifact can make startup fail; a missing artifact gives `/health` unhealthy and `/predict` 503.
- Parts of the generated reports (calibration and error-analysis narrative) are fixed text.
- Reasons use each category level's model coefficient, not a difference from the training average.
